# Furion Design tokens and structure

## Overview
A high-energy, athletic, purely greyscale aesthetic. Focused on dramatic motion, intense photography, and massive typography.

## Design Tokens

### Colors
- `--furion-black`: `#000000`
- `--furion-dark-grey`: `#222222`
- `--furion-grey`: `#777777`
- `--furion-light-grey`: `#eeeeee`
- `--furion-white`: `#ffffff`

### Typography
- `--furion-font-display`: `'Impact', 'Tungsten', 'Anton', sans-serif` (Bold, condensed, massive)
- `--furion-font-body`: `'Inter', sans-serif`

### Spacing & Borders
- `--furion-slant`: `-10deg` (Used for skewing typography or slicing images)
- Zero border radius, aggressive sharp corners.

## Component Inventory
1. **Action Hero**: Full width, intense black-and-white photo of an athlete, overlaid with massive slanted typography.
2. **Sliced Wordmark**: Typography with angled cuts (like the "A" in Furion).
3. **Three-Column Copy**: Clean, newspaper-like 3-column layouts for philosophical text blocks.
4. **Palette Card**: Simple visual displays of the greyscale progression.

## Layout Architecture
- Full-bleed imagery.
- Asymmetrical text placements.
- Heavy reliance on scale for hierarchy (massive titles vs tiny body copy).
